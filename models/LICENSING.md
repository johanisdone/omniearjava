# AudioDet — Licensing & Provenance Audit

This document records the licensing audit performed before publishing the
ShantyCam AudioDet artifacts. It classifies every data/model source that went
into the training recipe as **OURS-CLEAN** (publishable), **THIRD-PARTY**
(referenced only, never re-hosted here) or **NON-COMMERCIAL / NON-REDISTRIBUTABLE**
(must not leave the project under a permissive license).

Only the OURS-CLEAN column is published. Everything else is *cited as a
reference*, not redistributed.

## 1. What IS published (OURS-CLEAN)

| Artifact | What it is | License |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| `ShantyCam/audiodet-synth-smoke` (dataset) | 250 fully **synthetic** smoke-alarm T3 clips generated by `gen_smoke_alarms.py` (pure NumPy tone synthesis, no third-party audio) | CC-BY-4.0 |
| `recipe/*.py` | Our training / calibration / synthesis code | Apache-2.0 |
| `ced-mini-q8.gguf`, `ced-mini-f16.gguf` | CED-mini backbone weights, quantized to GGUF | Apache-2.0 (derivative of `mispeech/ced-mini`, see NOTICE) |
| `heads/v4_ced`, `heads/v4_distill` | Our MLP classifier heads (527→512→256→128→17) trained on frozen CED-mini embeddings | Apache-2.0 (our weights) |
| `onnx/head.onnx` (+ `.data`) | ONNX build of our head for the CED ONNX frontend | Apache-2.0 (our weights) |

### Backbone provenance
The CED-mini backbone is **Xiaomi CED** (Consistent Ensemble Distillation,
RicherMans/CED), redistributed on Hugging Face as `mispeech/ced-mini` under
**Apache-2.0**. Apache-2.0 permits redistribution and modification (quantization)
with attribution + NOTICE, which we provide. The canonical GGUF distribution of
the CED family is `mudler/ced-gguf` (LocalAI). The GGUF conversion tooling is
`ced.cpp` (localai-org/ced.cpp, **MIT**).

### Head provenance & transparency note
The classifier heads are our own trained weights. They were trained on frozen
CED-mini embeddings computed from a mixture of sources (see §2/§3), **including a
private ShantyCam camera-domain audio set that is NOT released** (GDPR — real
people/premises). Trained MLP weights over max-pooled 527-dim AudioSet logits are
a transformation, not a copy: no source audio and no personal data are
recoverable from them. We therefore publish the weights while keeping every
underlying audio corpus private/referenced. Per-class evaluation counts in
`report.json` are aggregate metrics only (no content, no identifiers).

## 3. NON-COMMERCIAL / NON-REDISTRIBUTABLE — MUST NOT ship permissively

| Source | License | Consequence |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| ESC-50 | **CC-BY-NC 3.0** | NC — no permissive redistribution of clips or derived mixes |
| UrbanSound8K | **CC-BY-NC 3.0** | NC — same |
| TUT Rare Sound Events 2017 (DCASE) | **non-commercial EULA** | NC — same |
| CryCeleb (Ubenwa) | research/agreement license | Restricted — reference only |
| FSD50K CC-BY-NC subset | **CC-BY-NC** | NC portion |

**Derived-mix rule:** any augmented clip produced by convolving/mixing a clip from
an NC source (ESC-50, UrbanSound8K, TUT Rare, FSD50K-NC) — including the RIR /
background-mix augmentations — inherits the NC restriction and is **not published**.
No such audio (raw or augmented) is in these repos. Only model weights (a
non-reversible transformation) are released.

## 4. PRIVATE — never leaves the project (GDPR)

`~/audiodet_review/{clips,bg_clips,export,rescued,aug_v3}`, `labels*.json`,
`manifest*`, `events.jsonl`, `raw_scores.jsonl`, embedding caches
(`emb_*.pkl`) — real recordings from ShantyCam cameras ("Auto"/"Terasa") with
identifiable people/premises. **Never published in any form.** The existing
`ShantyCam/audiodet` dataset repo (Hugging Face) that holds the mixed
training-fixture corpus, including AudioSet YouTube-derived audio, remains
**private** and must stay private.
